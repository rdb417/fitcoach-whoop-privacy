## Volkswagen Group relevance: how to handle it

The Volkswagen Group is the fund's sole LP, so partners will want to know when a company has genuine relevance to the Group's operations. It is equally important not to manufacture one.

Mention a Volkswagen Group angle only when there is a concrete workflow match, for example:
- Vehicle manufacturing operations: body shop, paint, final assembly, powertrain and component plants, logistics, quality.
- Battery cell manufacturing (the Group's battery company, PowerCo).
- Commercial vehicles (TRATON group brands).
- Vehicle software, charging and energy services.

Group structure changes; verify the entity names you use.

Rules:
- Phrase it as a hypothesis ("could be relevant to body-shop quality at Group plants {INF}"), never as access, a pilot, or a commitment.
- If the company already works with a Group brand, cite the source and say what the relationship is (supplier, pilot, partnership announcement).
- If there is no concrete match, leave Volkswagen out entirely. "Strong fit with the firm's industrial AI focus" is a complete answer.
- Fit also covers the firm's thesis and portfolio, not only the LP. Many good fits have nothing to do with automotive.
