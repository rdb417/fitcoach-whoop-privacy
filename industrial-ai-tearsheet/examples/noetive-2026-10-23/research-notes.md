## Seven questions (working notes)
- **Why now:** LLM-based agents can act across messy enterprise systems; cheap multimodal sensing; labor scarcity in plant planning roles. All general; company-specific evidence thin.
- **Why this team:** strong AI product and engineering leadership at scale (Meta, Google, Amazon); prior startup exit (Barak). No disclosed plant-operations founder.
- **Why this technology:** the claim is cross-customer self-improvement plus physical sensing. Unverified; no papers or technical docs found.
- **Why this market:** operational decisions in physical industries are a large budget pool, but the five verticals named are very different. Wedge appears to be production planning.
- **Why it might win:** outcome data compounding across sites; pod data that software-only players lack; strong investor network for enterprise access.
- **Why it might fail:** becomes forward-deployed services; incumbents (SAP, Kinaxis, o9, Palantir) add agents over the same data; hardware slows rollouts; breadth dilutes focus.
- **Why Leitmotif:** industrial AI thesis fit. Volkswagen Group: production planning and inbound logistics at component or PowerCo plants is a plausible hypothesis only; works-council approval would be needed for in-plant sensing in Germany.
