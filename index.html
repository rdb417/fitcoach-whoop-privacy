This privacy policy applies to the personal “Fit Coach” application that connects to WHOOP solely for Ryan Bayliss’s own health tracking.

What we access

With your consent, Fit Coach requests WHOOP API access to: recovery scores, physiological cycles / day strain, sleep, and workouts.

How we use it

Data is used only to log readiness metrics into Ryan’s personal Daily Scorecard / calendar and to guide training decisions. It is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.

Storage

Tokens and metrics may be stored on Fit Coach’s private computer and in Ryan’s Notion Health Hub / Google Calendar under his accounts.

Sharing

Only Ryan and his designated assistants (Fit Coach, Holistic Health Coach, Food Coach) may see summarized metrics. No third-party marketing sharing.

Retention & deletion

Data is kept while Ryan uses Fit Coach. He may revoke WHOOP access anytime in his WHOOP account and request deletion by emailing the contact above.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
